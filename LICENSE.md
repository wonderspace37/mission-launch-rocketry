## Using our designs

You can copy, change, build, fly and share them, including commercially, as long as you give credit. A line like
this is enough:

> Based on Yellow Jacket by Mission Launch Rocketry, Mission College
> (https://github.com/wonderspace37/mission-launch-rocketry), CC BY 4.0.

Say if you changed it. CC BY 4.0 comes with no warranty: a design that flew for us is not a promise that it is safe for
you. Check it yourself and fly under the NAR/Tripoli safety codes.
