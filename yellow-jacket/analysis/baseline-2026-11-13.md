# Yellow Jacket baseline review (2026-11-13)

What the Nov 13 OpenRocket file does with **every stage switched on** (the file itself ships with the booster off),
before the rework. All numbers are from OpenRocket 23.09, run headless by the scripts in
[`tools/openrocket/`](../../tools/openrocket).

## The rocket

58 mm (Always Ready Rocketry 2.15" tube), 1,468 mm long, 1,885 g at liftoff (1,363 g dry plus two 261 g motors).

| | |
|---|---|
| Booster | AeroTech H219T, 38 mm, 235 Ns, 1.08 s burn, 296 N peak |
| Sustainer | AeroTech H100W DMS, 38 mm, 228 Ns, 2.40 s burn |
| Staging | timer: separation and sustainer ignition both at launch + 4.0 s |
| Apogee | 1,563 m / 5,130 ft (2 m/s wind); max 191 m/s, Mach 0.56, 14.9 g |
| Rail exit | 16.7 m/s on the file's 1.0 m rod, 23.5 m/s on 6 ft, 26.7 m/s on 8 ft |
| Sustainer ignition | 337 m, 69.5 m/s, 1.7° from vertical (2 m/s wind) |
| Recovery | 18" apogee chute (12 m/s), 36" main at 457 m / 1,500 ft (5.4 m/s), 24" booster chute at separation (5.3 m/s) |

Static margin at Mach 0.3, 0° angle of attack, in 58 mm calibers:

| Configuration | Mass | CG | CP | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Full stack, liftoff | 1,885 g | 862 mm | 911 mm | **0.81** |
| Full stack, booster burnout | 1,741 g | 818 mm | 911 mm | 1.53 |
| Sustainer stack, ignition | 1,331 g | 666 mm | 712 mm | **0.80** |
| Sustainer stack, burnout | 1,210 g | 629 mm | 712 mm | 1.45 |
| Booster alone, burnt out | 409 g | 1,311 mm | 1,429 mm | 1.93 |

## Problems

1. **Off-rail stability.** OpenRocket's margin just after the rail, where crosswind gives an angle of attack:
   6 ft rail: 0.29 cal at 2 m/s, -0.04 at 4 m/s, -0.24 at 6 m/s. 1 m rod: 0.07 / -0.27 / -0.45. In the .ork the
   booster fins (60 mm root, 25 mm span) are smaller than the sustainer fins (60 × 36.5 mm).
2. **Sustainer margin is 0.80 cal at ignition**, with 120 g of nose weight already in.
3. **The booster chute deploys at separation**, at 69.5 m/s (about 155 mph). It should open at booster apogee.
4. **Main at 1,500 ft** drifts 287 / 600 / 950 m in 2 / 4 / 6 m/s wind.
5. The 4 s staging timer is fine for altitude. Lighting earlier is slightly *lower* (4,922 ft at 1.2 s) because the
   sustainer then peaks low in thick air. Tilt at ignition is 1.7° to 4.2° for 2 to 8 m/s wind.
6. Model gaps: the booster fins weigh 0 g, the av-bay window weighs 0 g, and there is no interstage coupler.

## The CAD fins don't match the .ork

Measured from the Onshape exports in [`../cad/onshape`](../cad/onshape) (all on a 57.2 mm printed body):

| | Root chord | Span | Fin can length |
|---|---|---|---|
| Booster, .ork | 60 mm | 25.0 mm | 90 mm |
| Booster, CAD (Lower Stage, Jun 30) | 75 mm | 32.0 mm | 76 mm |
| Sustainer, .ork | 60 mm | 36.5 mm | 150 mm |
| Sustainer, CAD (Upper Stage (Real), Feb 24) | 75 mm | 35.1 mm | 101 mm |

The same .ork with the CAD fin sizes (same triangle shape), 6 ft rail:

| Fins | Stack static | Sustainer static | Off rail 2 / 4 m/s | Apogee |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| .ork as saved | 0.81 | 0.80 | 0.29 / -0.04 | 5,130 ft |
| CAD booster fins | 1.82 | 0.80 | 1.09 / 0.63 | 5,112 ft |
| CAD booster and sustainer fins | 1.76 | 0.67 | 1.05 / 0.59 | 5,199 ft |

So which one is real decides whether the stack is marginal or fine, and either way the sustainer needs more fin.

## Fin span sweep (.ork shape, 60 mm root, 6 ft rail, extra fin area as solid PETG)

| Booster span | Sustainer span | Stack static | Sustainer static | Off rail 2 / 4 / 6 m/s | Apogee |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25 (file) | 36.5 (file) | 0.81 | 0.80 | 0.29 / -0.04 / -0.24 | 5,130 ft |
| 35 | 36.5 | 2.08 | 0.80 | 1.29 / 0.79 / 0.47 | 5,089 ft |
| 45 | 36.5 | 3.10 | 0.80 | 2.17 / 1.54 / 1.14 | 5,047 ft |
| 35 | 45 | 2.25 | 1.96 | 1.50 / 1.00 / 0.67 | 4,880 ft |
| 45 | 45 | 3.12 | 1.96 | 2.27 / 1.68 / 1.27 | 4,839 ft |
| 25 | 55 | 1.53 | 2.89 | 0.97 / 0.57 / 0.31 | 4,665 ft |

Margins move a lot with span because the fins are shorter than the body radius (29 mm). Most of the apogee lost to
bigger sustainer fins is drag from 5 mm square-edged fins, so rounded or airfoiled edges should win most of it back.
Flutter is not a concern at Mach 0.56 with these stubby fins.
