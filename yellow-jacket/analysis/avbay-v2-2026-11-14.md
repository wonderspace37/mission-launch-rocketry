# Av bay v2, printed nose cone v2, chute swap (2026-11-14)

Why: the COTS nose cone is out of stock, the av bay is due a rework (bigger battery, cleaner wiring, a camera window
the team is wrapping in clear sheet), and the motor's ejection charge would blow the main out of the upper stage near
apogee. This note covers what was found, what was decided and why, and what the OpenRocket model now says.

Drawings: [`../cad/avbay-v2/avbay-v2-drawing.png`](../cad/avbay-v2/avbay-v2-drawing.png) (sled both sides, section,
wiring) and [`../cad/nose-cone-v2/nose-cone-v2-drawing.png`](../cad/nose-cone-v2/nose-cone-v2-drawing.png).
CAD source: [`tools/cad/build_avbay_nose.py`](../../tools/cad/build_avbay_nose.py) (FreeCAD, every dimension at the top).

## 1. The current av bay

From the build photos and the club's components sheet:

- **Altimeter: Featherweight Blue Raven 2** (board dated 7/24/2025). Six screw terminals: `+In, Gnd, Apo, Main, 3rd, 4th`.
  Only Apo and Main are wired (lever nuts labelled APOGEE / MAIN).
- **Power: a 9 V battery** (45 g) through a round rocker switch, feeding the Raven and an LM2596 adjustable buck module
  (the "voltage regulator", 9.8 g) that powers the camera through wire nuts. A 12-way push-in splice block and lever
  nuts distribute power.
- **Camera: RunCam Thumb Pro W + 128 GB card**, taped to the sled, lens facing out through a hole in the mid collar.
- **Sled:** printed, about 50 × 240 mm, on two threaded rods. Airframe is Always Ready Rocketry 54 mm Blue Tube, painted.
- The club also bought an EasyMega and an EasyMini; neither is on this sled.

Problems with it: the rocker switch can flip under load; wire nuts, lever nuts and push-in splices are bulky and not
vibration-proof; the 9 V cell is heavy and above what Featherweight now recommends (next section); the sled needs a
~250 mm bay.

## 2. Spec sheets

**Blue Raven** ([product page](https://www.featherweightaltimeters.com/blue-raven-altimeter.html),
[user's guide, May 12 2026](https://www.featherweightaltimeters.com/uploads/1/0/9/5/109510427/blue_raven_users_manual_2026may12.pdf)):

- 1.8" × 0.8" (45.7 × 20.3 mm); same footprint, holes (#4-40) and terminals as the Raven4. Four outputs.
- **Battery: "1S (3V-4.2V) lipo are recommended."** The board is designed to tolerate 9 V, but the May 2026 guide says
  a battery over 4.2 V is *not recommended* because accidental contact with the higher voltage on exposed parts can
  permanently damage it. Large lipos are fine: the board limits output current to about 4 A.
- Outputs fire by switching to ground; the other e-match leg goes to battery + through the switch ("Arm+").
- Continuity detection needs at least 3.8 V on the pyro battery.
- Mount it rigidly, in any orientation. Vent the bay: several small holes are better than one big one. Apogee works
  even if the bay is sealed, **but the main will not fire without ambient pressure.**
- **The barometric sensor is sensitive to light**: don't let sunlight reach it. This matters with a clear window.
- Defaults: Apo + 3rd at apogee (3rd = backup), Main at 700 ft and 4th at 500 ft (backup).

**RunCam Thumb Pro W** ([RunCam spec page](https://runcam.com/products/runcam-thumb-pro-w)): 54 × 25.5 × 21 mm (the
drawing shows the lens block 24.5 mm tall, the body 13 mm), 16 g, **DC 5.0 V input, max 500 mA**, no battery, 4-pin
power/UART cable, Micro USB data, 155° FOV, up to 256 GB card.

**Pololu U3V16F5 5 V step-up** ([Pololu #4941](https://www.pololu.com/product/4941)): 1.3–16 V in (starts at 2.7 V),
13.1 × 8.1 × 3 mm, 0.4 g, 85–95 % efficient, no short-circuit or reverse protection.

**Switches:** Featherweight's [Magnetic Switch](https://www.featherweightaltimeters.com/store/p4/Magnetic_Switch.html)
(19 × 14 mm, 3.5–16 V, 12 A continuous, works through the airframe from ~1.8") and
[Power Perch](https://www.featherweightaltimeters.com/store/p5/Power_Perch.html) are both listed **Unavailable** today.

The 2S pack on the "Need to Buy" sheet (53.2 g) couldn't be checked: Amazon blocks automated reading.

## 3. Power: one 1S pack instead of 2S

The plan was a larger 7.2–7.4 V 2S pack straight into the Raven, plus a step-down to 5 V for the camera. Featherweight's
current guidance is 1S for the Blue Raven, and 2S (8.4 V charged) is above it, as is today's 9 V battery. So v2 keeps
the same architecture with the voltage flipped:

- **One 1S LiPo (1000 mAh, XT30), straight to the Raven** — what Featherweight recommends.
- **The camera gets its own branch:** switch → 1.1 A polyfuse → Pololu U3V16F5 → 5 V. The fuse matters because the
  boost has no short-circuit protection and the cell also fires the charges.
- **One switch arms everything.** At the pad the camera starts when you arm.

Endurance: the camera draws up to 2.5 W, about 2.8 W from the cell after the boost. A 1000 mAh cell (3.7 Wh) gives
roughly an hour of recording; a 1500 mAh cell up to 60 × 28 × 12 mm still fits. **Charge to 4.2 V before the launch.**
If the cell sags under 3.8 V after a long wait, the Raven's continuity beeps read low even though it would still fire.
If that becomes a problem, give the camera its own small 1S cell (fits on the window side, aft of the boost) and keep
the Raven's cell for flight.

If the team still wants 2S: swap the boost for a 5 V buck (e.g. Pololu D24V10F5, 5.1–36 V in, 1 A) and accept
Featherweight's warning. The sled doesn't care.

## 4. Wiring and connectors

See the wiring diagram on the drawing. Suggested parts:

| Joint | Use | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Battery lead | **XT30** | Polarised, 30 A, small, very tight fit; zip-tie or tape it in flight |
| Permanent harness | **Solder + heat shrink**, 22 AWG silicone | Replaces wire nuts, lever nuts and the splice block |
| Raven | its own screw terminals, **ferrules** on stranded wire | |
| Bulkheads | **screw terminal blocks** (2 × 2-pos or one 4-pos per bulkhead), wires through holes sealed with epoxy | Fast e-match hook-up at the pad; keeps charge gas out of the bay |
| Camera | the RunCam's own 4-pin cable, 5 V + GND only, soldered to the boost | No extra connector in the camera path |
| Switch | **screw switch** reached through a Ø4 hole in the collar, or a Featherweight magnetic switch when back in stock | Replaces the rocker switch, which can flip under load |

Use all four outputs with Featherweight's defaults: Apo + 3rd → the two charges in the drogue bay (aft bulkhead),
Main + 4th → the two charges in the main bay (fore bulkhead).

## 5. Sled v2

- Fits **one 147 mm LOC 54 mm coupler** (the club bought two), with 6.4 mm bulkhead disks inside each end:
  134 mm free, sled 126 mm. If the current coupler is the longer one in the model, trim it to 147 mm centred on the
  collar and keep the collar (and its camera hole).
- **Window side:** camera with its lens on the collar centre, held by rails, an end stop and two zip ties; boost + fuse.
- **Shaded side:** Blue Raven on 4 mm standoffs (its baro sensor faces the sled, so the plate itself blocks the window
  light), the switch under the collar, the 1S cell in a cradle with two zip ties.
- Fit check against a 50.3 mm bore: lens-block corners 1.2 mm clear, sled 1.1 mm, Raven 2.7 mm, no overlaps
  ([`fit.json`](../cad/avbay-v2/fit.json)).
- **Print:** PETG-CF, 3 walls, 30 % gyroid, flat on the window side, tree supports: **23.7 g**, 1 h 24 m, 6 g of support.
- The Raven hole positions were measured off a photo: check them against the board (`RAVEN_HOLES`) before printing.

**Clear wrap over the collar:** punch **three Ø2.0 vents at 90° / 210° / 330°** (calculator rule of thumb ~1.5 mm for
this bay, rounded up as it advises) and the **Ø4 switch access** at the bottom. The sheet seals the camera hole, which is
good: charge gas stays out of the bay.

## 6. Nose cone v2 (printed)

- **Von Kármán, 190 mm (3.3:1) + 55 mm shoulder, 245 mm overall.** Fits a 250 mm-tall Bambu bed, prints tip-up with no
  supports. Ø57.4 to match the Blue Tube; shoulder Ø54.0 for the 54.4 mm bore.
- **Shock-cord tether:** a **5/16" × 2" hardened dowel pin** across the shoulder, through a thickened internal band
  (6 mm of PETG-CF around the pin). Put the pin through a Kevlar loop or quick link, push it through both holes, then
  slide the shoulder into the tube. The tube traps the pin ends (1.6 mm under the surface). No glue joint carries load.
  The holes are teardrops (round on the loaded aft side) so they print clean; ream to 8.0 mm if tight.
- **Strength estimate**, design snatch load 1 kN (~225 lbf): pin bending > 5 kN; PETG-CF bearing ~3 kN;
  tear-out below the hole ~6 kN; shoulder wall in tension across layers ~6 kN. Weakest link about **3 kN (~700 lbf)**.
  These are estimates, so do a hang/yank test before flight.
- **Ballast:** pour lead shot + epoxy into the tip through the open shoulder (the 45° cavity ceiling holds it). Keep the
  model's **120 g**: the table below shows less isn't enough with today's fins.
- **Print:** PETG-CF, 5 walls, 15 % gyroid: **83.5 g**, 2 h 39 m (printmass).
- Drill the shear-pin holes (2 × #2-56) through tube and shoulder at assembly, in the band (10–34 mm from the open end),
  90° from the pin.

## 7. Recovery: main up, drogue down

- **Main (36") moves into the nose section** above the av bay, fired by the Raven's Main/4th. Nose shoulder and av-bay
  coupler leave about 180 mm of the 299 mm tube for the chute, cord and protector.
- **Drogue (18") moves into the upper-stage tube** below the av bay, fired by the Raven's Apo/3rd. The H100W's
  ejection charge (14 s delay, about 2 s after apogee) fires into this bay too, so it becomes a third, independent
  apogee backup instead of a premature main. If the team would rather leave that bay empty as planned, the alternative
  is main + drogue together in the nose bay with a chute release (e.g. Jolly Logic).
- Starting charges (ideal-gas sizing at ~25 psi for two #2-56 shear pins): main bay (~26 in³) ≈ 0.35 g BP, drogue bay
  (~30 in³) ≈ 0.4 g; backups +25 %. **Ground-test both bays**, as the team already does.
- The .ork still deploys the main at **1,500 ft**; the Raven default is **700 ft**. Pick one and make them match.
  700 ft cuts the drift in a 4 m/s wind from 554 m to 455 m.

## 8. Measured mass

The upper stage with parachute and loaded motor weighs **729.43 g**; the model said **666.0 g** (+63.4 g). The model's
666 g is exactly its "2nd Stage": tube, PETG coupler, fin can, fins, 90 g main and loaded H100W. Where the extra 63 g
sits isn't known, so it's added spread over the stage. Variant D below puts it all in the fin can as a worst case.
**Find the stage's balance point** and the model can place it properly.

## 11. Measure before printing

Blue Tube OD and ID, coupler ID, the Raven's hole positions, the 1S cell you buy, the collar's camera-hole size, and the
upper stage's balance point. Edit the numbers at the top of `build_avbay_nose.py` and rerun it; the drawings, fit check
and STLs all regenerate. The masses marked EST in `avbay_v2_update.py` should be replaced with scale readings.
